# Program Roadmap

## Purpose

Complete a deliberate refresher in the four foundational areas DIU currently lists as undergraduate preparation for advanced applied-linguistics work, using Egyptian Arabic as the main application language and preserving evidence of mastery.

## Phase 1 — AL4302 Phonetics

**Modeled credits:** 3  
**Target hours:** 135  
**Status:** Active

Exit criteria:
- practical IPA recognition and production;
- broad and selected narrow transcription;
- articulatory descriptions from first principles;
- basic analysis in Praat;
- preliminary Egyptian Arabic phonetic corpus and description;
- baseline, midterm, final practical, and oral defense completed.

## Phase 2 — AL4303 Phonological Analysis

**Modeled credits:** 3  
**Target hours:** 135

Planned focus:
- etic vs. emic analysis;
- complementary distribution and contrast;
- natural classes and distinctive features;
- phonological processes and conditioning environments;
- syllable structure, stress, tone where relevant;
- morphophonemics;
- Egyptian Arabic phonological sketch grounded in Phase 1 data.

## Phase 3 — AL4410 Grammatical Analysis

**Modeled credits:** 4  
**Target hours:** 180

Planned focus:
- word classes and allomorphy;
- inflection and derivation;
- Arabic root-and-pattern morphology as one application area;
- phrase, clause, and sentence analysis;
- progressively developed Egyptian Arabic grammar sketch.

## Phase 4 — AA4305 Second Language and Culture Acquisition

**Modeled credits:** 3  
**Target hours:** 135

Planned focus:
- learner-directed acquisition;
- participation with a native speaker/community;
- language-learning strategy design and revision;
- cultural observation and reflection;
- documented growth in Egyptian Arabic communicative competence.

## Cross-program research thread

Each course contributes evidence to a longitudinal question:

> Where can modern AI responsibly accelerate language analysis and Bible translation, and where does it still require skilled human linguistic judgment, native-speaker participation, and community oversight?

## Graduate-study handoff

At the end of the four studies, produce a single portfolio index containing:
- documented hours;
- competency matrix;
- assessments and scores;
- final projects;
- research notes;
- public instructional artifacts;
- AI experiment reports;
- a reflective statement describing remaining gaps before formal graduate work.
