# Program Roadmap

## Purpose

Complete a deliberate refresher in the four foundational areas DIU currently lists as undergraduate preparation for advanced applied-linguistics work, using Egyptian Arabic as the main longitudinal field language while preserving cross-linguistic breadth and evidence of mastery.

## Program scale

| Phase | Modeled credits | Target hours | Designed length |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AL4302 Phonetics | 3 | 135 | 12 weeks + baseline |
| AL4303 Phonology | 3 | 135 | 12 weeks |
| AL4410 Grammatical Analysis | 4 | 180 | 16 weeks |
| AA4305 Language & Culture Acquisition | 3 | 135 | 12 weeks |
| **Total** | **13** | **585** | **~52 instructional weeks + transitions** |

The independent studies do not claim DIU credit. They model scope, workload, assessment, and evidence so the work is valuable whether or not any institution recognizes it formally.

## Phase 1 — AL4302 Phonetics

**Status:** designed / first study  
**Exit evidence:** practical IPA recognition/production; transcription; speech-mechanism knowledge; basic Praat analysis; preliminary Egyptian Arabic phonetic description; baseline/midterm/final/oral defense.

**Handoff to AL4303:** verified phonetic data, uncertain contrasts, variation notes, candidate processes, acoustic measurements, and intentionally unresolved phonological questions.

## Phase 2 — AL4303 Phonological Analysis

**Status:** designed  
**Exit evidence:** cross-linguistic phonemic analyses; distinctive features/natural classes; processes/conditioning; phonotactics; tone practicum; morphophonemics; orthography memo; preliminary Egyptian Arabic phonological sketch; AI phonology benchmark.

**Handoff to AL4410:** stable transcription/phonological conventions, morphophonemic findings, corpus identifiers, and a cleaner basis for morpheme segmentation.

## Phase 3 — AL4410 Grammatical Analysis

**Status:** designed  
**Exit evidence:** cross-linguistic morphology/syntax analyses; Egyptian Arabic paradigms/interlinear examples; nonlinear morphology case study; constituency/grammatical-relation analyses; complex-sentence project; preliminary grammar sketch; AI grammar-induction benchmark.

**Handoff to AA4305:** linguistic knowledge becomes a diagnostic resource, not the learning method. Grammar/phonology may help identify problems, but communicative participation becomes primary.

## Phase 4 — AA4305 Second Language and Culture Acquisition

**Status:** designed  
**Exit evidence:** minimum 24 hours of native-speaker acquisition lab; strategy experiments; listening/speaking growth portfolio; culture-learning portfolio; AI language-coach study; baseline/final comparison; sustainable continuation plan.

## Cross-course rules

1. **Arabic depth, typological breadth.** Egyptian Arabic is longitudinal field data; graded cross-linguistic analysis prevents overfitting.
2. **Analysis first.** For original language-analysis questions, human work is frozen before published descriptions or AI are consulted.
3. **Evidence lineage.** Session/token IDs should survive across courses so later claims can trace back to source data.
4. **Uncertainty survives.** An unresolved question is a valid artifact; unsupported certainty is not.
5. **Native-speaker respect.** The consultant supplies language and perspective, not responsibility for the student's linguistic theory.
6. **AI as research object and assistant.** AI may tutor and be benchmarked; it does not manufacture graded human work or replace community/native-speaker judgment.

## Longitudinal research thread

Each course contributes evidence to:

> Where can modern AI responsibly accelerate language acquisition, language analysis, and Bible-translation workflows, and where does it still require skilled human linguistic judgment, native-speaker participation, and community oversight?

Progression:
- AL4302: AI and phonetic/speech analysis.
- AL4303: AI and phonological inference.
- AL4410: AI and morphology/syntax induction.
- AA4305: AI and real-world language/culture learning.

## Program completion portfolio

At the end of all four studies, produce one portfolio containing:
- 585+ documented hours with no padding;
- course competency matrices;
- baseline/midterm/final assessments;
- remediation history;
- major Egyptian Arabic descriptions/projects;
- representative cross-linguistic problem sets;
- public instructional artifacts;
- research bibliography/notes;
- AI experiment reports and longitudinal synthesis;
- data/ethics statement;
- final reflective statement identifying remaining gaps before formal graduate work.
