# Egyptian Arabic Independent Study

A self-directed applied-linguistics preparation program modeled on the four undergraduate prerequisites Dallas International University (DIU) currently lists for advanced applied-linguistics preparation.

The project uses **Egyptian Arabic** as the primary longitudinal field language while maintaining deliberate cross-linguistic breadth. The long-term purpose is to rebuild rigorous foundations in phonetics, phonology, grammatical analysis, and second-language/culture acquisition before graduate study, while developing a parallel research thread on responsible uses of AI in language analysis and Bible translation.

## Program roadmap

| Study | DIU analogue | Credits modeled | Hours | Primary focus |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | AL4302 Principles of Articulatory and Acoustic Phonetics | 3 | 135 | IPA, articulation, transcription, suprasegmentals, acoustic analysis |
| 2 | AL4303 Principles of Phonological Analysis | 3 | 135 | contrast, distribution, features, processes, tone, morphophonemics |
| 3 | AL4410 Principles of Grammatical Analysis | 4 | 180 | morphology, word classes, phrases, clauses, syntax |
| 4 | AA4305 Second Language and Culture Acquisition | 3 | 135 | learner-directed acquisition and cross-cultural participation |
| **Total** |  | **13** | **585** |  |

These independent studies are **not DIU courses and do not claim DIU credit**. They approximate scope, rigor, workload, assessment, and evidence so the work is worthwhile regardless of whether an institution recognizes it formally.

## Repository principles

1. **Mastery over speed.**
2. **Evidence over self-assessment.**
3. **Human-first academic work.**
4. **Field-language depth plus cross-linguistic breadth.**
5. **Reproducible research habits.**
6. **Respect for the language consultant.**
7. **Uncertainty is documented rather than hidden.**
8. **A living, version-controlled curriculum.**

## Public/private boundary

This repository is public. It stores curriculum, original notes, public-safe metadata, analysis methods, rubrics, research findings, and approved portfolio artifacts. It should **not** store raw private recordings, signed consent forms, personal contact information, copyrighted textbook scans, private assessment keys before use, or private AI exports containing sensitive speaker data.
