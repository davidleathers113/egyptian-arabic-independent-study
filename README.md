# Egyptian Arabic Independent Study

A self-directed applied-linguistics preparation program modeled on the four undergraduate prerequisites used by Dallas International University (DIU) for advanced study in applied linguistics and Bible translation.

The project uses **Egyptian Arabic** as the primary field language while maintaining a deliberately cross-linguistic perspective. The long-term purpose is to rebuild rigorous foundations in phonetics, phonology, grammatical analysis, and second-language/culture acquisition before graduate study, while developing a parallel research thread on responsible uses of AI in language analysis and Bible translation.

## Program roadmap

| Study | DIU analogue | Credits modeled | Primary focus |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | AL4302 Principles of Articulatory and Acoustic Phonetics | 3 | IPA, articulation, transcription, suprasegmentals, acoustic analysis |
| 2 | AL4303 Principles of Phonological Analysis | 3 | Phonemic analysis, distribution, processes, tone, morphophonemics |
| 3 | AL4410 Principles of Grammatical Analysis | 4 | Morphology, word classes, phrases, clauses, syntax |
| 4 | AA4305 Second Language and Culture Acquisition | 3 | Learner-directed acquisition and cross-cultural participation |
| **Total** |  | **13** | **~585 hours using a 45-hours-per-credit planning standard** |

The independent studies are **not DIU courses and do not claim DIU credit**. They are designed to approximate the scope, rigor, evidence, and workload of university-level study so that the resulting portfolio demonstrates genuine preparation regardless of whether any institution recognizes it formally.

## Repository principles

1. **Mastery over speed.** The goal is to learn the material deeply, not to imitate a transcript.
2. **Evidence over self-assessment.** Every competency should point to exercises, recordings, analyses, exams, or projects.
3. **Human-first academic work.** AI may tutor, critique, and help design experiments, but it may not produce graded work on the student's behalf.
4. **Field-language depth + cross-linguistic breadth.** Egyptian Arabic is the application language, not the limit of the course.
5. **Reproducible research habits.** Methods, metadata, assumptions, and revisions should be documented.
6. **Respect for the language consultant.** Private audio, identifying information, and consent records do not belong in this public repository unless separately and explicitly approved for publication.
7. **Living curriculum.** Research findings and improved methods should be incorporated through version-controlled revisions.

## Public/private boundary

This repository is public. It stores curriculum, original notes, public-safe metadata, analysis code, rubrics, research findings, and portfolio artifacts. It should **not** store raw private recordings, signed consent forms, personal contact information, copyrighted textbook scans, answer keys for future assessments, or private AI exports containing sensitive speaker data.

See [`PRIVACY-AND-DATA.md`](PRIVACY-AND-DATA.md) for the full policy.
