# Final Project — Preliminary Phonological Sketch

Working title:

**A Preliminary Phonological Sketch of Egyptian Arabic Speech from SPK001**

The project is speaker/sample-specific. It does not claim to describe all Egyptian Arabic.

## Target length

Approximately 25–40 polished pages plus appendices/data tables.

## Required sections

1. **Scope and speaker/sample** — what data are represented and what are not.
2. **Methods** — inherited AL4302 corpus, new elicitation, transcription conventions, analysis workflow.
3. **Phonetic inventory vs. proposed phonemic inventory** — keep the distinction explicit.
4. **Contrast evidence** — representative minimal/near-minimal or analogous-environment evidence.
5. **Allophony and distribution** — proposed allophones with environments and counterevidence.
6. **Distinctive features/natural classes** — only where analytically useful.
7. **Syllable structure and phonotactics** — permitted patterns and limitations of the sample.
8. **Phonological processes** — process, conditioning, examples, confidence.
9. **Stress/larger-domain observations** — as supported by data.
10. **Morphophonemics** — at least one developed alternation analysis.
11. **Variation and unresolved questions** — do not force uncertain data into a clean system.
12. **Orthographic implications** — limited analytical observations, not a proposed community orthography.
13. **Comparison with published descriptions** — completed only after the independent analysis was frozen.
14. **Future elicitation plan** — what evidence would most improve the analysis.

## Evidence standard

Every substantive language-specific claim should be traceable to tokens, paradigms, session IDs, or cited external literature. A statement such as “Egyptian Arabic does X” is too broad unless the external literature supports the variety claim; otherwise describe what SPK001/the study corpus shows.

## Appendices

Recommended:
- phoneme/phone tables;
- contrast tables;
- distribution tables;
- selected paradigms;
- hypothesis ledger export;
- public-safe corpus index;
- change log showing analyses revised after published comparison.
