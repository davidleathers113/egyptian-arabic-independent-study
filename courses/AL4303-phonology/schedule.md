# AL4303 — 12-Week Schedule

Target: **135 hours total** (~11.25 hours/week). Adjust individual weeks as needed while preserving the total and the required outputs.

## Week 1 — From phones to phonemes
- Review AL4302 corpus and freeze inherited data.
- Study etic vs. emic evidence; phoneme/allophone concepts.
- Complete cross-linguistic classification problems.
- Build Egyptian Arabic hypothesis ledger: `observation → possible analysis → evidence needed`.
- **Output:** baseline phonological analysis of 20–30 inherited tokens, explicitly marked provisional.

## Week 2 — Contrast and minimal evidence
- Contrast in identical and analogous environments.
- Minimal and near-minimal pairs; lexical evidence limits.
- Design elicitation to test 3–5 Egyptian Arabic candidate contrasts.
- **Output:** contrast table + consultant elicitation session.

## Week 3 — Complementary distribution and variation
- Complementary distribution, overlapping distribution, free variation.
- Practice environment notation.
- Reanalyze AL4302 variants without assuming they are allophonic.
- **Output:** distributional analysis report.

## Week 4 — Distinctive features and natural classes
- Feature systems; natural classes; economy vs. overfitting.
- Cross-linguistic feature problems.
- Map candidate Egyptian Arabic consonant/vowel classes.
- **Output:** feature matrices and natural-class memo.

## Week 5 — Processes and conditioning environments
- Assimilation, dissimilation, deletion, epenthesis, lenition/fortition, metathesis where represented in datasets.
- Formalize inputs, outputs, and environments.
- Test several Egyptian Arabic connected-speech observations.
- **Output:** process-analysis packet.

## Week 6 — Syllables, phonotactics, neutralization
- Syllable constituents, permissible sequences, distributional restrictions.
- Neutralization/underspecification at an introductory level.
- Build a preliminary Egyptian Arabic phonotactic inventory from corpus data.
- **Midterm practical:** unseen datasets covering contrast, distribution, features, and processes.

## Week 7 — Stress and larger domains
- Word-level vs. phrase/utterance-level patterning.
- Stress evidence; relation to phonetic measurements from AL4302.
- Avoid treating orthographic word boundaries as automatically phonological.
- **Output:** stress/domain analysis memo.

## Week 8 — Tone analysis practicum
- Tone-bearing units, level/contour tone, tone contrasts, basic autosegmental insight.
- Use one or more non-Arabic datasets with genuine lexical/grammatical tone.
- **Output:** independent tone-analysis practical and corrected version.

## Week 9 — Morphophonemics I
- Alternations across morphological contexts.
- Separate phonologically conditioned, lexically conditioned, and morphologically conditioned alternations.
- Begin Egyptian Arabic alternation case study.
- **Output:** alternation dataset + first analysis.

## Week 10 — Morphophonemics II and underlying representation
- Competing underlying forms; economy; abstractness; evidence limits.
- Cross-linguistic morphophonemic problem sets.
- Revise Egyptian Arabic case study.
- **Output:** 6–10 page morphophonemic case study.

## Week 11 — Orthography and AI benchmark
- Translate phonological findings into orthographic questions without assuming phonemic spelling is always optimal.
- Write a hypothetical orthography decision memo for one limited issue.
- Run the same unseen phonology datasets through selected AI systems **after** human grading.
- **Outputs:** orthography memo + AI benchmark report.

## Week 12 — Synthesis
- Complete preliminary Egyptian Arabic phonological sketch.
- Audit every claim for supporting tokens/evidence.
- Identify unresolved analyses and next elicitation needs.
- **Final practical:** unseen cross-linguistic datasets.
- **Oral defense:** defend 3–5 major analytical decisions and at least one decision you remain uncertain about.
