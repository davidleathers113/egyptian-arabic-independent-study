# AL4303 Assessment Plan

## Baseline
Before Week 1, analyze one unfamiliar short dataset without notes/AI. Preserve the original response.

## Weekly problem sets — 25%
At least 10 substantial cross-linguistic datasets across the course. Require written reasoning, not answers alone.

## Egyptian Arabic labs — 15%
Evidence tables, distribution maps, elicitation design, process hypotheses, and revisions.

## Midterm practical — 10%
Closed-AI, limited-reference. Unseen datasets testing:
- contrast;
- complementary distribution;
- free variation;
- distinctive features/natural classes;
- process/environment formulation;
- phonotactics.

## Tone practicum — 10%
Analyze an unfamiliar tonal dataset sufficiently rich to require contrast and pattern reasoning. Complete human analysis before viewing any key.

## Morphophonemic case study — 10%
6–10 pages plus data appendix. Must separate surface alternations, conditioning, proposed underlying forms, and competing analyses.

## Orthography memo — 5%
2–4 pages on one hypothetical orthography decision. Explain phonological evidence plus non-phonological considerations that would still require community input.

## AI benchmark — 5%
Compare independently graded human analyses with AI analyses of selected unseen datasets. Score accuracy, unsupported assumptions, missed evidence, and confidence calibration.

## Final phonological sketch — 15%
See `projects/final-phonological-sketch.md`.

## Final practical + oral defense — 5%
A cumulative unseen analysis plus a recorded defense of major Egyptian Arabic analytical decisions.

## Mastery requirement

Target: 85% overall. Additionally, the student must demonstrate mastery in:
- contrast/distribution reasoning;
- process/conditioning analysis;
- morphophonemics.

If any core domain is weak, remediate and retest it even if the numerical average is above 85%.
