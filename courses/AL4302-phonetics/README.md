# AL4302 Independent Study — Phonetics

**Title:** Principles of Articulatory & Acoustic Phonetics — Egyptian Arabic  
**Modeled credits:** 3  
**Workload:** 135 hours  
**Format:** 12 instructional weeks plus Week 0 baseline  
**Primary field language:** Egyptian Arabic  
**Cross-linguistic requirement:** yes

## Start sequence

1. Read [`syllabus.md`](syllabus.md).
2. Read the integrity and privacy policies at the repository root.
3. Complete [`weeks/week-00-baseline.md`](weeks/week-00-baseline.md) before intensive phonetics review.
4. Enter baseline results in `progress/assessment-results.md` and time in `progress/hours-log.csv`.
5. Begin Week 1.

## Course components

- systematic reading across five phonetics references;
- public university/IPA lecture resources;
- articulatory practice and ear training;
- weekly IPA transcription;
- native-speaker field sessions;
- Praat acoustic analysis;
- two teach-back videos;
- academic writing;
- midterm and final practicals;
- final phonetic description;
- one formal AI evaluation experiment.

## Minimum pass standard

The self-imposed mastery threshold is **85% overall**, with separate remediation required for any essential practical skill that remains below standard.
