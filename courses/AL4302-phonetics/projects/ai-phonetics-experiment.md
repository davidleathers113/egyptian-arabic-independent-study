# AI Experiment — Field Phonetics Evaluation

## Question

What can a current AI system reliably contribute to introductory field-phonetic work with Egyptian Arabic, and where does it overstate certainty or substitute orthographic expectations for acoustic/phonetic evidence?

## Design

Select approximately 10–20 de-identified examples for which the student has already frozen a human analysis. Depending on tool capabilities, provide audio, waveform/spectrogram images, transcriptions, or measurements.

Ask the AI to perform a narrowly defined task such as:
- propose an IPA transcription;
- describe an articulation;
- interpret a measured contrast;
- identify uncertainty or needed evidence.

## Evaluation categories

- correct/supported;
- useful but incomplete;
- partially correct;
- unsupported inference;
- orthography-driven assumption;
- hallucinated precision;
- missed relevant evidence.

## Native-speaker role

The consultant may judge naturalness or confirm the elicited forms, but should not be asked to validate phonetic theory he has not been trained in.

## Deliverable

1,000–1,500 word report plus a reproducible prompt/input log, anonymized outputs where publication is appropriate, and a conclusion about safe/unsafe AI roles.
