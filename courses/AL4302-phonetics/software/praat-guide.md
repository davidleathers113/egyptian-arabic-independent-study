# Praat Working Guide

This guide grows with the course. It is intentionally task-oriented rather than a full Praat manual.

Official documentation: https://www.fon.hum.uva.nl/praat/manual/Intro.html

## Week 1–2: open and inspect audio

1. Open Praat.
2. Use **Open → Read from file...** to load a working WAV file.
3. Select the Sound object and choose **View & Edit**.
4. Practice zooming, selecting intervals, replaying short sections, and reading timestamps.
5. Do not alter the archival master file.

## Week 7–8: TextGrid annotations

For selected working audio, create a TextGrid with only the tiers you actually need. Begin with:
- `utterance`
- `arabic`
- `broad_IPA`
- `notes`

Add `narrow_IPA`, `stress`, or `phonetic_event` only when the analysis justifies them.

Save TextGrids separately from audio and keep the base session ID identical.

## Week 9: waveform and spectrogram

Learn to explain:
- what the waveform represents;
- what time is on the horizontal axis;
- what frequency is on the spectrogram's vertical axis;
- why darkness/intensity patterns are not themselves linguistic labels.

Practice selecting a short interval and relating what you hear to visible acoustic events.

## Week 9–10: pitch and formants

Enable pitch/formant displays and learn to recognize obviously implausible tracking. Automatic tracks are hypotheses generated by algorithms, not ground truth.

For every measurement record:
- token/session ID;
- time point or interval;
- settings if they differ from the normal lab standard;
- whether the automatic track was manually inspected.

## Week 10: vowel measurements

For controlled tokens, measure duration and selected F1/F2 values using a consistent protocol. Decide the measurement point in advance (for example, a stable midpoint where appropriate) rather than choosing the visually most convenient location after seeing results.

Use `templates/phonetic-measurements.csv`.

## Week 11: consonant mini-analysis

Select one or two phenomena and define the measurement procedure before collecting values. Examples may include duration, stop timing, or selected spectral observations.

## Reproducibility notes

When an analysis becomes part of a graded/project artifact, record:
- Praat version;
- input file/session ID;
- analysis settings that materially affect results;
- segmentation rule;
- measurement rule;
- exclusions and why they were excluded.

## Scripting

Do not automate a measurement until you can perform and audit it manually. Later scripts should output inspectable data and never overwrite human annotation without review.
