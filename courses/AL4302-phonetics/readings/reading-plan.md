# AL4302 Reading Plan

## Principle

Do not assign fake page numbers across unknown editions. When each physical/ebook edition is in hand, record its table of contents and map exact chapters/pages to this topical plan.

## Roles

- **McKinney & McKinney:** systematic field-phonetics backbone.
- **Catford:** active articulatory experiments; produce the sounds, not just definitions.
- **Small:** IPA/transcription drills and alternate explanations.
- **Mayer:** respiration, phonation, voice, articulation, and speech mechanism reference.
- **Ladefoged & Johnson:** broader academic and acoustic reference.

## Topical mapping

| Week | Topics to locate across the books |
|---|---|
| 1 | phonetics domains; anatomy; speech mechanism; IPA overview |
| 2 | airstream, phonation, stops, place and manner |
| 3 | fricatives, affricates, uvular/pharyngeal/glottal regions |
| 4 | secondary articulations and IPA diacritics |

## Reading response rule

For substantial assigned chapters/articles, record:
- citation;
- 200–400 word summary;
- 5–10 key concepts;
- unresolved questions;
- application to field/Bible-translation work;
- optional AI/NLP connection.
