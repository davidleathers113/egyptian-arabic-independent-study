| **Total** | **100%** |

## Self-imposed pass standard

- **85–100:** mastery/pass
- **80–84.9:** conditional; remediate weak competencies and reassess
- **below 80:** not yet complete

## Essential-skill override

A high weighted average cannot compensate for failure to demonstrate an essential skill. Before course completion, the student must separately demonstrate acceptable performance in:
- basic IPA recognition and production;
- broad transcription;
- articulatory description;
- basic Praat navigation/measurement;
- ethical data handling.

## Revision policy

Essays/projects may be revised after feedback. Baseline attempts are never overwritten. Midterm/final raw attempts are preserved alongside remediation evidence.
