# Final Practical and Oral Defense

## Final practical

Demonstrate, under closed-AI conditions:
1. auditory identification and IPA transcription of unfamiliar phones;
2. production from IPA prompts;
3. articulatory explanation from first principles;
4. broad transcription of unfamiliar connected speech;
5. selected justified narrow transcription;
6. recognition of major suprasegmental observations;
7. basic Praat analysis on a previously unseen recording;
8. measurement of duration and selected pitch/formant features;
9. cautious explanation of what the acoustic evidence can and cannot support.

## Baseline repeat

Repeat the Week 0 benchmark as closely as practicable and produce a side-by-side error/competency comparison.

## Oral defense — 30–45 minutes

Topic: **What I Understand About Human Speech After 135 Hours of Phonetic Study**

Address:
- major concepts;
- hardest changes in perception/production;
- consultant-data method;
- final project claims and limitations;
- acoustic findings;
- mistakes that changed your thinking;
- what belongs in phonology rather than phonetics;
- implications for future AI/Bible-translation work.

Follow with prewritten challenge questions answered without notes.
