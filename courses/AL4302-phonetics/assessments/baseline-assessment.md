# Week 0 Baseline Assessment

Complete **before intensive phonetics review**. Preserve the raw attempt unchanged.

## Part A — IPA recognition

Use a preselected set of 20 IPA symbols covering familiar and unfamiliar places/manners/vowels. For each, state what you think the sound is and attempt production.

## Part B — Production

Record yourself attempting 20 target phones from IPA prompts. Do not practice them immediately before recording.

## Part C — Vocal-tract explanation

From memory, draw and label the speech mechanism and explain place, manner, voicing, and airflow in your current terminology.

## Part D — Egyptian Arabic transcription

Record or select approximately 30 seconds of consultant speech. Attempt a broad phonetic transcription without first searching for a published transcription of the passage.

## Part E — Praat baseline

Open the same recording in Praat and document what you can currently identify or measure. It is acceptable for the answer to be "I do not yet know."

## Part F — Self-prediction

Write one page:
- strongest current area;
- weakest current area;
- sounds you expect to be hardest;
- what you think acoustic analysis will add;
- what you want Hour 135 to look like.

## Freeze rule

Save all baseline materials as `baseline_v0`. Do not correct them. At the end of Week 12 repeat the tasks and compare the two performances.
