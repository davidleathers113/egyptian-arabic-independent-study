# AL4302 Master Schedule

Target: **135 total documented hours**. The weekly distribution below is a planning guide; mastery and actual logged work control the final record.

| Week | Focus | Primary outputs |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Baseline | frozen pre-study IPA/transcription/articulation/Praat benchmark |
| 1 | What phonetics is; vocal tract; IPA orientation | vocal-tract diagram, first coach corpus, short reflection |
| 2 | Stops, airflow, voicing, place/manner | consonant drills, cross-language stop lab, corpus metadata |
| 3 | Fricatives/affricates/posterior articulations | Arabic-focused lab, teach-back video 1 |
| 4 | Secondary articulation/emphasis/diacritics | narrow-transcription practice, Essay 1 |
| 5 | Vowels | vowel-production/recognition drills, controlled coach dataset |
| 6 | Broad transcription integration | midterm practical |
| 7 | Narrow transcription and allophonic detail | re-transcription of early corpus, correction analysis |
| 8 | Suprasegmentals | stress/length/pitch/intonation lab, teach-back video 2 |
| 9 | Acoustic foundations | Praat waveform/spectrogram/pitch/formant labs |
| 10 | Acoustic vowel analysis | preliminary vowel-space report |
| 11 | Acoustic consonant analysis + AI experiment | measurement report; AI evaluation memo |
| 12 | Integration | final phonetic description, final practical, oral defense |

## Typical 11.25-hour instructional week

- Reading: 2.0 h
- Structured lecture/tutorial: 1.25 h
- Articulation/ear training: 2.0 h
- IPA/transcription exercises: 2.0 h
- Consultant session: 1.0 h
- Egyptian Arabic/acoustic project: 2.0 h
- Notes/log/reflection: 1.0 h

Adjust by week: assessment/project weeks may shift reading time into analysis or writing.
