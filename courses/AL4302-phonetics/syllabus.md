# Syllabus — AL4302 Principles of Articulatory & Acoustic Phonetics — Egyptian Arabic

## Course purpose

Reproduce the core intellectual and practical preparation represented by DIU's public AL4302 description while applying the skills intensively to Egyptian Arabic and preserving cross-linguistic breadth.

## Workload

- 135 documented hours
- approximately 11.25 hours/week across 12 instructional weeks
- Week 0 baseline is included in the overall 135-hour budget; later weeks should be adjusted if necessary so the final total remains 135 or higher without padding hours.

## Learning domains

1. Speech anatomy and mechanisms.
2. IPA symbol knowledge.
3. Consonant production/perception.
4. Vowel production/perception.
5. Suprasegmentals.
6. Broad and selected narrow transcription.
7. Acoustic phonetics in Praat.
8. Ethical recording and field-consultant practice.
9. Cross-linguistic listening.
10. Cautious description of Egyptian Arabic speech data.

## Required working library

- McKinney & McKinney, *An Introduction to Field Phonetics* — field-phonetics backbone.
- Catford, *A Practical Introduction to Phonetics*, 2nd ed. — articulatory experimentation.
- Small, *Fundamentals of Phonetics: A Practical Guide for Students* — structured IPA/transcription practice.
- Mayer, *Fundamentals of Voice and Articulation* — voice and articulatory mechanics reference.
- Ladefoged & Johnson, *A Course in Phonetics* — broad academic/acoustic reference.

Exact chapters/pages are recorded in `readings/reading-plan.md` after matching the editions actually in hand.

## Required public resources

- International Phonetic Association resources.
- UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive for cross-linguistic listening.
- MIT OpenCourseWare phonetics material where appropriate.
- Praat official tutorial/manual.

## Practical emphasis

Aim for **at least 60% active phonetics**: listening, production, transcription, elicitation, analysis, measurement, assessment, and project work. Reading/lecture consumption should support practice, not replace it.

## Assessments

- Week 0 baseline.
- Weekly transcription/articulation labs.
- Midterm practical around Week 6.
- Two teach-back videos.
- Acoustic vowel/consonant mini-projects.
- Final phonetic description.
- Final practical and oral defense.

## Major final product

**A Preliminary Field-Phonetic Description of Egyptian Arabic Speech from SPK001**

The title and claims must reflect the actual speaker/sample rather than pretending to describe every Egyptian Arabic variety.

## Academic integrity

Root-level `ACADEMIC-INTEGRITY.md` governs all work.

## Data ethics

Root-level `PRIVACY-AND-DATA.md` governs consultant data. Raw recordings remain private unless separately approved for publication.
