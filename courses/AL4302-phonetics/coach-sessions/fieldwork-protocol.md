# Native-Speaker Fieldwork Protocol

## Role

The Egyptian Arabic coach serves as a **language consultant and conversation partner**. He supplies natural speech, repetitions when requested, contextual explanations, and native-speaker judgments. He is not responsible for knowing IPA or validating the student's linguistic theory.

## Before recording

- confirm the consent level for that session;
- state the session purpose;
- verify recording equipment/settings;
- note environment/noise;
- use pseudonymous speaker ID in public notes.

## Elicitation sequence

For isolated items where repetition is useful:
1. natural production;
2. brief pause;
3. natural repetition;
4. brief pause;
5. careful production only if analytically needed.

Avoid drilling until speech becomes artificial.

## Connected speech

Include short stretches where the consultant speaks without interruption. Use prompts such as a recent event, a familiar process, or description of a picture/story. Obtain separate permission before asking about personal topics.

## Consultant judgments

When testing possible contrasts or forms:
- do not pressure the consultant toward a predicted answer;
- distinguish "possible," "natural," "same meaning," "different meaning," and "I would not say it";
- record uncertainty and variation.

## After session

- duplicate master audio into secure backup locations;
- create a working copy;
- complete metadata promptly;
- log unresolved questions for a future session rather than over-interpreting memory.
