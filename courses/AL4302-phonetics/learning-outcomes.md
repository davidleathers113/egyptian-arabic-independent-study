# AL4302 Learning Outcomes

By completion, the student should be able to:

1. Identify and label major structures of the vocal tract.
2. Explain pulmonic airflow, phonation, place, manner, and relevant secondary articulations.
3. Read and write the IPA needed for broad cross-linguistic phonetic work.
4. Produce and recognize a broad inventory of consonant and vowel types.
5. Reliably attempt key articulations encountered in Egyptian Arabic without treating Arabic spelling as phonetic evidence.
6. Produce broad IPA transcriptions from recorded speech.
7. Use selected narrow diacritics when the added detail is justified.
8. Identify and represent stress, duration, pitch, and intonational observations at an introductory level.
9. Distinguish phonetic observation from phonological interpretation.
10. Collect reproducible speech data ethically from a native speaker.
11. Use Praat to inspect waveform, spectrogram, pitch, formants, and durations.
12. Take basic acoustic measurements and explain their limitations.
13. Produce a preliminary speaker-specific field-phonetic description.
14. Compare personal observations cautiously with published descriptions.
15. Explain foundational phonetics clearly through teach-back artifacts.

## Mastery evidence

A competency is not marked `mastered` because a reading was completed. It requires a linked artifact or assessment result in `progress/competencies.md`.
