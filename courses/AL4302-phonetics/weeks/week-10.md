# Week 10 — Acoustic Vowel Analysis

Use the controlled consultant dataset from Week 5.

For selected tokens measure, where methodologically appropriate:
- duration;
- F1;
- F2;
- F0/context if useful;
- measurement point;
- surrounding consonants and stress/context.

Plot a preliminary vowel space. Inspect outliers manually; never delete values merely because they look inconvenient.

Deliverable: **Egyptian Arabic Vowel Study — Preliminary Acoustic Analysis** (roughly 6–10 pages plus figures/data).
