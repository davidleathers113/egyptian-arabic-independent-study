# Week 6 — Broad Transcription and Midterm

Focus on integrating consonant/vowel knowledge into broad phonetic transcription.

Practice:
- short English samples;
- Egyptian Arabic samples;
- unfamiliar-language samples from reputable archives;
- timed transcription to reduce dependence on unlimited replay.

Complete the midterm practical under the conditions in `assessments/midterm-specification.md`.

After scoring, create a remediation list for Weeks 7–12 organized by **error type**, not by vague impressions.
