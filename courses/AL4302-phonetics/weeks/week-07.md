# Week 7 — Narrower Transcription and Revision

Study justified phonetic detail: aspiration, devoicing, nasalization, dentalization, retraction/advancement, syllabicity, duration, and other diacritics encountered in real data.

Key exercise: return to selected Week 1 recordings and create a new transcription without looking at the original. Then compare Week 1 and Week 7 analyses.

Deliverable: correction memo categorizing what improved because of better hearing, better symbol knowledge, better articulatory understanding, or better restraint.
