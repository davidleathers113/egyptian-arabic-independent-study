# Week 3 — Fricatives, Affricates, and Posterior Articulations

Study fricative/affricate mechanisms and posterior places relevant cross-linguistically, with Egyptian Arabic as the main applied dataset.

Practice:
- auditory discrimination across multiple languages;
- production drills for unfamiliar posterior articulations;
- compare student's production with consultant tokens;
- use spectrogram viewing descriptively without over-interpreting it yet.

Consultant session: collect words/phrases containing targeted sounds in controlled and natural contexts.

Deliverable: **Teach-back Video 1 — How Linguists Describe Consonants: Voicing, Place, and Manner** (about 8–12 minutes).
