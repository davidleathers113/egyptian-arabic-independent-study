# Week 2 — Stops, Voicing, Airflow, Place and Manner

Study pulmonic airflow, voicing/phonation, oral stops, release, aspiration, and major places of articulation.

Practice:
- produce stop series across several places;
- identify unfamiliar stop contrasts from cross-linguistic recordings;
- describe each target using consistent terminology;
- begin systematic consonant entries in the field corpus.

Consultant session:
- collect controlled tokens useful for stop/place/voicing observation;
- obtain natural ×2 and careful ×1 only when needed;
- record environment and context.

Deliverable: consonant-production audit identifying phones that are reliable, unstable, or not yet producible.
