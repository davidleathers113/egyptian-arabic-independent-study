# Week 8 — Suprasegmentals

Study stress, duration, rhythm, pitch, intonation, and boundaries as phonetic observations.

Consultant session: gather statements, questions, focus/contrast contexts, repeated words in sentence contexts, and connected speech where natural.

Practice:
- annotate stress/duration observations;
- inspect pitch contours cautiously;
- separate what is heard/measured from claims about grammatical or phonological function.
