# Week 4 — Secondary Articulation, Emphasis, and Diacritics

Study pharyngealization, velarization, palatalization, labialization, coarticulatory effects, and selected IPA diacritics.

Arabic application:
- collect controlled examples commonly described with emphatic/non-emphatic environments where naturally available;
- describe auditory/articulatory observations without jumping to phonemic conclusions;
- note possible vowel effects for later acoustic testing.

Deliverables:
- narrow-transcription practice set;
- **Essay 1: The Human Vocal Tract as an Instrument: How Speech Sounds Are Classified and Produced** (1,500–2,000 words).
