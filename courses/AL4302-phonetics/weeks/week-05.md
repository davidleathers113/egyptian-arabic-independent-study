# Week 5 — Vowels

Study vowel height, backness, rounding, duration, monophthongs/diphthongs, and the vowel quadrilateral.

Practice:
- production/recognition drills across a wider vowel space than English/Arabic alone;
- IPA vowel transcription;
- reference-vowel exercises;
- distinguish auditory judgment from later acoustic evidence.

Consultant session: collect a controlled Egyptian Arabic vowel dataset with repetitions and documented contexts.

Deliverable: preliminary perceptual vowel chart plus measurement plan for Week 10.
