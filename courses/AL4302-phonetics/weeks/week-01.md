# Week 1 — Speech Mechanism and IPA Orientation

## Core questions
What is phonetics? How is it different from phonology? How does the human vocal tract produce speech? What does IPA encode?

## Study
- Locate introductory speech-mechanism/IPA chapters in McKinney.
- Use Mayer for respiration/phonation/articulation reinforcement.
- Use Catford for hands-on articulatory exploration.
- Consult Small/Ladefoged & Johnson for alternate explanations.
- Work through introductory IPA/phonetics material from official/university resources.

## Practice
- draw and label the vocal tract from memory;
- produce/identify an initial set of IPA symbols;
- create sagittal sketches for several articulations;
- begin a cross-linguistic listening log.

## Consultant session
Collect a small word/sentence/self-introduction sample using the recording protocol. Attempt transcription before consulting published descriptions.

## Deliverables
- labeled vocal-tract diagram;
- first structured lecture log;
- first reading response;
- short reflection: **Hearing Speech vs. Analyzing Speech**.
