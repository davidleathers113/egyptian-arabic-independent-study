# AA4305 Reading and Resource Plan

This course should not become a reading-heavy SLA survey. Theory exists to improve the learner's actual acquisition program.

## Core practical influence — Growing Participator Approach (GPA)

Use reputable GPA materials and demonstrations to study language learning as growing participation in relationships and culture, with heavy use of comprehensible interaction rather than grammar explanation alone.

SIL's English with Newcomers materials are based on the Growing Participator Approach developed by Greg and Angela Thomson and emphasize internalizing language in relational/contextual participation. Although those materials target English learners, the underlying activity principles can inform Egyptian Arabic lab design.

Topics to extract:
- nurturer/participant roles;
- comprehension-first activities;
- use of objects, pictures, actions, and stories;
- increasing complexity;
- participation in culture rather than language as a detached code.

## Descriptive proficiency framework — ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines 2024

Use the public ACTFL framework to understand proficiency in terms of real-world functions/tasks, accuracy, context/content, and text type across listening, speaking, reading, and writing.

Important restriction: self-assessment or course assessment may describe performance using these concepts but must not claim to issue an official ACTFL proficiency rating.

## SLA/culture-learning research

Build a targeted reading set around:
- comprehensible input and interaction;
- noticing and attention;
- vocabulary acquisition;
- corrective feedback;
- pronunciation/perception;
- learner strategies and self-regulation;
- affect/anxiety/willingness to communicate;
- intercultural learning and ethnographic observation.

Prefer peer-reviewed articles, university texts, and SIL/mission language-learning resources with clear authorship.

## Weekly reading-response question

For every major reading ask:
1. What does this claim learners should do differently?
2. What evidence supports it?
3. How could I test it during Egyptian Arabic acquisition?
4. What result would make me abandon or modify the strategy?

## Language resources vs. academic resources

Egyptian Arabic dictionaries, graded content, videos, podcasts, and teaching materials may support acquisition but should be logged separately from academic SLA readings. The course assesses the ability to build an acquisition system, not consumption of a particular commercial curriculum.
