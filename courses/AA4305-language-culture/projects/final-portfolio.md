# Final Project — Language and Culture Acquisition Portfolio

## Purpose

Demonstrate that the student can build and run a learner-directed acquisition program and can show real, ethically documented growth in Egyptian Arabic participation.

## Required sections

1. **Starting point** — baseline evidence and existing study history.
2. **Needs analysis** — people, contexts, tasks, and long-term goals that matter.
3. **Acquisition plan v1** — initial weekly strategy.
4. **Native-speaker lab record** — hours, activity types, adaptations, evidence.
5. **Listening development** — representative before/mid/after samples.
6. **Speaking/interaction development** — spontaneous task samples and repair behavior.
7. **Vocabulary/phrase acquisition** — systems tried and evidence of retention/use.
8. **Pronunciation development** — only selected high-value issues, informed by AL4302/AL4303.
9. **Grammar discovery** — examples of useful noticing without making grammar analysis the dominant activity.
10. **Culture-learning journal synthesis** — hypotheses, corrections, relationships, misunderstandings, and multiple perspectives.
11. **Strategy experiments** — what was tested, evidence, decisions.
12. **AI experiment** — what AI did well/poorly compared with native-speaker evidence.
13. **Baseline vs. final comparison** — comparable unrehearsed tasks.
14. **Remaining limitations** — candid breakdown points and situations still outside ability.
15. **6–12 month continuation plan** — sustainable frequency, activities, people, materials, and measurable checkpoints.

## Evidence bundle

Where consent permits, include private references to audio/video session IDs. Public repository artifacts should use de-identified summaries, transcripts, measurements, or separately approved excerpts.

## Final reflection questions

- What can I now do spontaneously that I could not do at baseline?
- What learning behaviors produced the most improvement per hour?
- Where did formal linguistic knowledge help acquisition?
- Where did analysis distract from participation?
- What did I misunderstand culturally and how did my interpretation change?
- How dependent am I on one speaker, one topic, or one learning setting?
- What is the next participation frontier?
