# Syllabus — AA4305 Second Language and Culture Acquisition — Egyptian Arabic

## Course purpose

Learn how to direct one's own second-language and culture acquisition through meaningful participation, native-speaker relationships, reflection, strategy selection, and iterative adaptation.

## Workload

- 135 documented hours
- 12 instructional weeks
- target average: 11.25 hours/week
- substantial direct Egyptian Arabic participation every week

## Core domains

1. Learner-directed language acquisition.
2. Goal setting and needs analysis.
3. Comprehensible input and negotiated interaction.
4. Listening before and alongside production.
5. Vocabulary and phrase learning in meaningful contexts.
6. Pronunciation/phonological awareness informed by AL4302/AL4303.
7. Grammar discovery informed by AL4410 without turning every interaction into analysis.
8. Culture learning through participation, observation, questions, and relationships.
9. Strategy selection, experimentation, and revision.
10. Managing ambiguity, affect, mistakes, and communication breakdown.
11. Developing spontaneous listening/speaking ability.
12. Ethical and respectful participation with native speakers/communities.

## Core resources

- DIU's public AA4305 scope as the boundary.
- Growing Participator Approach (GPA) concepts and activity models as a major practical influence.
- ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines 2024 used only as a descriptive framework for real-world functions, accuracy, contexts/content, and text type; no self-assigned result should be represented as an official ACTFL rating.
- Additional high-quality SLA/culture-learning readings selected during course buildout.

## Practicum

Minimum **24 hours** of documented native-speaker acquisition-lab interaction across the course, ideally ~2 hours/week, separate from ordinary tutoring when possible.

The practicum should progressively move from highly supported comprehension toward richer participation, narratives, everyday tasks, and culturally meaningful interaction.

## Assessment model

- acquisition-lab participation/evidence: 25%
- learning journal and strategy experiments: 15%
- listening/speaking portfolio: 15%
- culture-learning portfolio: 15%
- reading/theory responses: 10%
- mid-course strategy audit: 5%
- AI language-coach experiment: 5%
- final portfolio + oral reflection: 10%

## Mastery standard

85% overall plus demonstrated ability to design and revise an acquisition plan based on evidence. Progress is evaluated against the student's baseline rather than requiring a predetermined official proficiency level.

## Academic integrity

AI can support planning, practice, feedback, and experiment design, but it cannot replace native-speaker participation or manufacture evidence of communicative growth.
