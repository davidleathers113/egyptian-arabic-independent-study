# AA4305 Assessment Plan

## Baseline portfolio
Capture unrehearsed samples of current ability before the course:
- spontaneous self-introduction/conversation;
- picture description;
- short listening comprehension task;
- narrative/retell if feasible;
- reading/writing only if relevant to current goals;
- learner needs/strategy inventory.

## Acquisition-lab participation — 25%
Minimum 24 documented hours. Grade preparation, learner-direction, activity appropriateness, reflection, and adaptation—not the native speaker's performance.

## Learning journal and strategy experiments — 15%
At least six explicit strategy experiments using a simple cycle: hypothesis → method → evidence → keep/modify/drop.

## Listening/speaking portfolio — 15%
Comparable samples across the course showing growth in spontaneous comprehension, interaction, repair, utterance/text length, and communicative range.

## Culture-learning portfolio — 15%
Observations, cultural hypotheses, multiple-perspective checks, misunderstandings/revisions, and participation reflections. Avoid stereotype-style lists.

## Reading/theory responses — 10%
Apply SLA/culture-learning readings to practical decisions.

## Mid-course strategy audit — 5%
At Week 6 compare current evidence with Week 1 goals and produce acquisition plan v2.

## AI language-coach experiment — 5%
Evaluate selected AI uses against native-speaker evidence for linguistic accuracy, naturalness, register, cultural appropriateness, and practical usefulness.

## Final portfolio + oral reflection — 10%
Repeat comparable baseline tasks and produce a documented 6–12 month continuation plan.

## Mastery criteria

Target 85% overall. No particular unofficial proficiency label is required. Mastery depends on:
- real documented participation;
- evidence of growth;
- ability to diagnose and revise learning strategy;
- mature handling of culture and generalization;
- sustainable independent-learning plan.
