# AA4305 Independent Study — Second Language and Culture Acquisition

**Modeled DIU course:** AA4305 Second Language and Culture Acquisition  
**Modeled credits:** 3  
**Target workload:** 135 documented hours  
**Primary language:** Egyptian Arabic

## Purpose

Develop a deliberate, learner-directed approach to acquiring language and culture through native-speaker participation, reflection, strategy experiments, functional evidence, and repeated redesign.

DIU publicly describes AA4305 as learner-directed second language and culture acquisition with emphasis on beginning stages, perspectives on language/culture learning, strategies for cultural differences, and a practical lab in a small-group, non-instructed setting with a native speaker.

## Course architecture

The deep curriculum uses four complementary lenses:

1. **SLA research** — Lightbown & Spada as the academic backbone.
2. **Growing participation** — GPA principles for relational/contextualized native-speaker work.
3. **Balanced acquisition** — Nation's Four Strands to balance input, output, language-focused work, and fluency.
4. **Culture fieldwork** — participant-observation discipline adapted from Spradley so cultural learning is evidence-based rather than stereotype-based.

ACTFL 2024's FACT dimensions are used only to describe functional performance; this independent study does not issue official ACTFL proficiency ratings.

## Special design for the Egyptian Arabic coach relationship

Distinguish two modes:

1. **Normal coaching/tutoring** — the coach may explain, teach, correct, translate, or structure lessons.
2. **Acquisition lab / nurturer mode** — the student directs meaningful activities while the coach participates primarily as a native speaker, interaction partner, storyteller, and source of speaker-specific/cultural perspective.

The second mode is the closest analogue to DIU's public non-instructed native-speaker lab description.

Minimum independent-study practicum: **24 documented acquisition-lab hours** across the course. This is our design floor, not a claim about DIU's unpublished lab-hour requirement.

## Major outputs

1. Baseline functional language/culture profile.
2. Personal acquisition plan v1 and v2.
3. 24+ hours documented acquisition-lab sessions.
4. Six strategy experiments.
5. Listening/speaking functional growth portfolio.
6. Four-Strands audits.
7. Communication-repair and corrective-feedback policy.
8. Learner-created acquisition activity library.
9. Culture-learning hypothesis ledger and case studies.
10. Mid-course strategy audit.
11. AI language-coach experiment.
12. Final functional evidence, oral defense, and 6–12 month continuation plan.

## Mastery standard

The course is not passed by memorizing vocabulary or explaining SLA theory.

Mastery requires evidence that the student can:

`identify need → design participation → interact → repair → observe → test strategy → evaluate evidence → seek host perspective → revise → participate again`

while showing genuine growth in unrehearsed Egyptian Arabic participation and increasingly mature cross-cultural learning.
