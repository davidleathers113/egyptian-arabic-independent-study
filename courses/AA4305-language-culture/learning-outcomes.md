# AA4305 Learning Outcomes

By the end of the study, the student will be able to:

1. Conduct a realistic needs analysis for language/culture learning.
2. Set functional goals that describe what the learner wants to understand/do, not merely what grammar/vocabulary to cover.
3. Design learner-directed sessions with a native speaker without requiring that person to be a trained teacher.
4. Use objects, pictures, actions, stories, routines, and contextual cues to make unfamiliar speech more comprehensible.
5. Gradually increase spontaneous listening and speaking demands while avoiding dependence on English translation.
6. Track vocabulary/phrases in ways tied to people, situations, and communicative functions.
7. Diagnose recurring pronunciation/comprehension problems using prior phonetic/phonological training when helpful.
8. Notice grammatical patterns without allowing linguistic analysis to crowd out communication.
9. Identify communication strategies for asking clarification, repairing misunderstandings, and continuing interaction.
10. Observe cultural behavior without prematurely assigning motives or value judgments.
11. Form cultural hypotheses, seek multiple perspectives, and revise interpretations.
12. Reflect on expectations, frustration, identity, power, relationship, and adaptation in cross-cultural learning.
13. Evaluate which personal learning strategies produce measurable gains and discontinue ineffective ones.
14. Document growth using repeated comparable listening/speaking tasks and real-world communication evidence.
15. Use a descriptive proficiency framework responsibly without claiming an unofficial assessment is an official rating.
16. Evaluate where AI supports language practice and where it diverges from native-speaker language, cultural judgment, or relational participation.
17. Produce a sustainable post-course Egyptian Arabic learning plan.

## Evidence requirement

Outcomes involving communication or culture must be supported by participation evidence, not only essays about theory.
