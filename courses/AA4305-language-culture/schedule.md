# AA4305 — 12-Week Schedule

Target: **135 hours total** (~11.25 hours/week), including at least 24 hours of acquisition-lab interaction with native speaker(s).

## Week 1 — Baseline and needs analysis
- Record baseline spontaneous speaking and listening tasks.
- Inventory existing Egyptian Arabic exposure, strengths, gaps, motivations, and real-world goals.
- Distinguish language knowledge from communicative ability.
- Establish journal, interaction log, and privacy rules.
- **Output:** baseline portfolio + 12-week acquisition plan v1.

## Week 2 — Comprehensible participation
- Study learner-directed/comprehensible-input principles.
- Use highly contextualized objects/actions/pictures with the coach in nurturer mode.
- Minimize English during lab portions.
- **Output:** session analysis identifying what made speech comprehensible.

## Week 3 — Listening systems
- Build routines for repeated listening without turning all audio into transcription homework.
- Use short recordings/stories from the coach.
- Track gist, details, and breakdown points.
- **Output:** repeated-listening growth sample.

## Week 4 — Interaction and repair
- Develop Egyptian Arabic strategies for clarification, repetition, confirmation, and signaling misunderstanding.
- Run communication tasks in which successful repair matters more than grammatical perfection.
- **Output:** repair-strategy inventory + recorded task.

## Week 5 — Vocabulary through participation
- Compare isolated memorization with vocabulary learned through activities, stories, people, and recurring situations.
- Build a personal high-frequency/need-based lexical system.
- **Output:** strategy experiment comparing two learning approaches.

## Week 6 — Pronunciation in communication
- Use AL4302/AL4303 skills selectively to diagnose persistent pronunciation/perception problems.
- Avoid making every conversation a phonetics lab.
- Re-record selected baseline speaking tasks.
- **Mid-course audit:** compare evidence and revise acquisition plan.

## Week 7 — Grammar discovery without grammar domination
- Notice recurring constructions from real interaction.
- Use AL4410 skills to formulate private hypotheses, then return focus to communication.
- Design activities that elicit/use a construction naturally.
- **Output:** form-function discovery memo.

## Week 8 — Stories and connected discourse
- Picture sequence, personal narrative, retell, and comprehension activities.
- Move beyond isolated sentences.
- Track ability to follow and produce connected speech.
- **Output:** narrative portfolio sample.

## Week 9 — Culture as lived participation
- Select recurring cultural domains relevant to real relationships: hospitality, politeness, family/social expectations, humor, time, requests, disagreement, religious/social language, etc., only as naturally encountered.
- Observe before generalizing; seek multiple perspectives when possible.
- **Output:** cultural hypothesis log with revisions.

## Week 10 — Real-world tasks
- Complete practical tasks or simulations primarily in Egyptian Arabic: planning, describing a problem, asking directions/information, arranging something, explaining preferences, recounting events.
- Choose tasks based on actual learner goals.
- **Output:** task-performance evidence and breakdown analysis.

## Week 11 — AI language-coach experiment
- Compare AI-generated Egyptian Arabic, corrections, explanations, role-play, or speech feedback with native-speaker judgments.
- Separate linguistic correctness from naturalness, register, cultural appropriateness, and relationship.
- **Output:** AI language-coach evaluation report.

## Week 12 — Final growth evidence and next plan
- Repeat comparable baseline tasks without rehearsal.
- Compare listening, speaking, interaction, text length, repair ability, and comfort.
- Complete final culture-learning reflection.
- Create 6–12 month continuation plan.
- **Final oral reflection:** explain what strategies worked, failed, changed, and why.

## Typical weekly allocation

| Activity | Hours |
|---|---:|
| Acquisition lab / native-speaker participation | 2.0 |
| Independent listening/speaking practice | 2.5 |
| Reading/theory | 1.5 |
| Vocabulary/phrase review in context | 1.5 |
| Culture participation/observation | 1.0 |
| Portfolio/journal/analysis | 1.5 |
| Targeted pronunciation/grammar support | 1.25 |
| **Total** | **11.25** |
