# AL4410 Assessment Plan

## Problem sets — 30%
At least 14 substantial graded datasets across morphology and syntax. At least half use non-Arabic languages. Require reasoning and correction, not answer-only submissions.

## Egyptian Arabic labs — 15%
Paradigms, interlinear examples, elicitation design, hypothesis ledger, and revised analyses.

## Morphology midterm — 10%
Closed-AI unseen datasets covering segmentation, classes, allomorphy, inflection, derivation, nonlinear morphology, and word/clitic issues.

## Syntax midterm — 10%
Closed-AI unseen datasets covering constituency, grammatical relations, valence, phrases, clause patterns, and complex constructions.

## Root-and-pattern case study — 5%
A focused analysis of selected Egyptian Arabic verbal/nominal patterns that explicitly discusses where a simple concatenative model succeeds or fails.

## Complex-sentence project — 5%
Analyze connected and elicited Egyptian Arabic data involving at least two types of clause combination or embedding.

## AI grammar-induction benchmark — 5%
Compare independently graded human analyses with AI analyses on selected unfamiliar morphology and syntax datasets.

## Final grammar sketch — 15%
See `projects/final-grammar-sketch.md`.

## Final practical + oral defense — 5%
Mixed unseen data plus defense of major Egyptian Arabic analyses.

## Mastery standard

Target 85% overall. Separately require demonstrated competence in:
- morphological segmentation/allomorphy;
- inflection/derivation;
- constituency;
- grammatical relations/argument structure;
- clause/complex-sentence analysis.

Remediate and reassess weak core domains regardless of average.
