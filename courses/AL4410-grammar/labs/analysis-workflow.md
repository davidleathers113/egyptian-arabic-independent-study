# AL4410 Grammatical Analysis Workflow

Use this workflow for unfamiliar morphology/syntax datasets and Egyptian Arabic field data.

## 1. Preserve forms and contexts
Do not normalize away irregular forms, clitic boundaries, word order, repetition, or apparent exceptions.

## 2. Separate form, meaning, and function
Create explicit tables linking observed forms to glosses/contexts before assigning categories.

## 3. Propose segmentation cautiously
Identify recurrent pieces and state alternative segmentations when evidence is weak. Do not force nonconcatenative Arabic morphology into a purely prefix/suffix model.

## 4. Build paradigms
Use paradigms to expose distribution, gaps, allomorphy, agreement, and irregularity.

## 5. Establish categories from behavior
For word classes and syntactic categories, use distributional/morphological diagnostics. Semantic labels are clues, not proof.

## 6. Distinguish levels of analysis
Keep separate:
- morpheme/allomorph;
- word/clitic;
- phrase/constituent;
- grammatical relation;
- semantic role;
- discourse/pragmatic effects.

## 7. Form hypotheses
For each proposal document:
- data explained;
- diagnostic used;
- counterexamples;
- alternatives;
- confidence;
- next evidence needed.

## 8. Design discriminatory elicitation
Do not merely translate English sentences. Use pictures, contrasts, substitution frames, paradigms, acceptability/naturalness checks, story retells, and contextualized prompts as appropriate.

## 9. Interlinearize consistently
For selected examples maintain:
1. original utterance;
2. morpheme segmentation;
3. morpheme-by-morpheme gloss;
4. free translation;
5. source/session ID;
6. notes on uncertainty.

Adopt a consistent gloss-abbreviation list and document deviations.

## 10. Compare externally only after freezing analysis
Published grammars and AI can challenge an analysis after the first independent version is saved. Preserve the revision trail.

## Hypothesis ledger

`ID | construction | data | hypothesis | diagnostics | counterevidence | next elicitation | confidence | status`

Statuses: `open`, `testing`, `provisional`, `supported`, `rejected`, `unresolved`.
