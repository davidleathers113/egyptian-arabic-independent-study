# AL4410 — 16-Week Schedule

Target: **180 hours total** (~11.25 hours/week).

## Week 1 — Grammatical analysis from data
- Review basic terminology without assuming universality.
- Morphemes, morphs, hypotheses, data integrity.
- Cross-linguistic segmentation problems.
- Begin Egyptian Arabic paradigm notebook.

## Week 2 — Word classes
- Language-specific diagnostics for nouns, verbs, adjectives, adpositions, particles, etc.
- Compare semantic labels with distributional evidence.
- **Output:** word-class analysis of an unfamiliar dataset + provisional Egyptian Arabic categories.

## Week 3 — Allomorphy
- Identify recurrent form/meaning relationships.
- Conditioned allomorphy vs. suppletion vs. phonological alternation.
- Link back to AL4303 morphophonemics.
- **Output:** allomorphy problem set and Egyptian Arabic mini-paradigms.

## Week 4 — Inflection
- Person/number/gender, case, agreement, tense/aspect/mood as represented in datasets.
- Build normalized paradigm tables without erasing irregularity.
- **Output:** inflectional-system analysis.

## Week 5 — Derivation and valence
- Category-changing/meaning-changing derivation.
- Causative/applicative or other valence phenomena where represented.
- **Output:** cross-linguistic derivation packet.

## Week 6 — Nonlinear morphology
- Root-and-pattern/nonconcatenative morphology.
- Egyptian Arabic becomes the main application lab.
- Compare concatenative and nonconcatenative analytical assumptions.
- **Project:** Egyptian Arabic root-and-pattern case study.

## Week 7 — Words, clitics, and boundaries
- Diagnostics for wordhood; clitics vs. affixes; orthography is not decisive evidence.
- Analyze selected Egyptian Arabic bound elements.
- **Output:** word-boundary/clitic memo.

## Week 8 — Morphology midterm + synthesis
- Closed-AI unseen morphology datasets.
- Remediation plan if needed.
- Begin final grammar-sketch morphology chapters.

## Week 9 — Constituent structure
- Constituents, syntactic categories, phrase structure, evidence.
- Analyze unfamiliar phrase data.
- **Output:** constituent-analysis packet.

## Week 10 — Grammatical relations and argument structure
- Subject/object-like relations, semantic roles, transitivity, valence.
- Avoid projecting English diagnostics onto unfamiliar languages.
- Egyptian Arabic elicitation: controlled argument-structure sets.

## Week 11 — Noun phrases and modifiers
- Determination, possession, adjectives/modifiers, agreement, embedding within NP.
- **Output:** Egyptian Arabic noun-phrase mini-description + cross-linguistic problem set.

## Week 12 — Clause patterns and constituent order
- Basic clauses; variable orders; pragmatic vs. grammatical influences as data permit.
- Negation introduced.
- **Output:** clause inventory and evidence tables.

## Week 13 — Questions, commands, negation
- Interrogative strategies, imperatives, negative constructions.
- Consultant elicitation must prioritize natural contexts over English sentence translation alone.
- **Output:** construction comparison memo.

## Week 14 — Complex sentences
- Coordination, complement clauses, adverbial clauses, relative clauses.
- **Project:** complex-sentence analysis using connected Egyptian Arabic data plus controlled elicitation.

## Week 15 — Syntax midterm + AI benchmark
- Closed-AI unseen syntax datasets.
- Run selected morphology/syntax datasets through AI only after human grading.
- **Outputs:** syntax midterm + AI grammar-induction benchmark.

## Week 16 — Grammar sketch and final defense
- Complete preliminary grammar sketch.
- Audit interlinear examples and terminology.
- Separate observed generalizations from tentative claims.
- **Final practical:** mixed unseen morphology/syntax data.
- **Oral defense:** explain and defend major analyses, including at least one unresolved issue.

## Typical weekly allocation

|---|---:|

| Grammar-sketch/project writing | 2.0 |

| **Total** | **11.25** |
