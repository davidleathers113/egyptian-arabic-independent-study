# Final Project — Preliminary Grammatical Sketch

Working title:

**A Preliminary Grammatical Sketch of Egyptian Arabic Speech from SPK001**

## Target length

Approximately 40–60 polished pages plus appendices.

## Required sections

1. **Scope, variety, speaker/sample, and methods**.
2. **Conventions** — transcription, segmentation, gloss abbreviations, example numbering.
3. **Word classes** — evidence-based categories and uncertain cases.
4. **Nominal morphology** — number/gender/definiteness/possession or other supported categories.
5. **Pronouns and pronominal elements**.
6. **Verbal morphology** — paradigms, stem/root-pattern behavior, agreement, tense/aspect/mood distinctions as supported.
7. **Derivation and selected word formation**.
8. **Clitics and word-boundary issues**.
9. **Noun phrase structure**.
10. **Basic clause structure and grammatical relations**.
11. **Argument structure and valence**.
12. **Negation**.
13. **Questions and commands**.
14. **Selected complex sentences** — coordination, complementation, adverbial or relative clauses as data allow.
15. **Variation and unresolved analyses**.
16. **Comparison with published Egyptian Arabic descriptions** — only after independent chapters were frozen.
17. **Future elicitation/research plan**.

## Example standard

Important claims should include numbered examples with:
- source utterance;
- morpheme segmentation when justified;
- morpheme glosses;
- natural English translation;
- session/token ID;
- uncertainty note where applicable.

## Analytical standard

The goal is not comprehensive coverage of Egyptian Arabic. It is a transparent, evidence-backed grammatical sketch showing that the student can move from raw data to defensible morphological and syntactic description.

## Appendices

Recommended:
- paradigm tables;
- gloss abbreviation list;
- construction inventory;
- hypothesis ledger;
- public-safe corpus/example index;
- major revision log after external comparison.
