# Sources and Reference Standards

Prefer primary/official sources for claims about DIU requirements, IPA conventions, software behavior, and SIL resources. Secondary sources may supplement them.

## DIU

- DIU nontraditional/distance course listings: https://www.diu.edu/online-courses/
- DIU academic programs/course schedule: https://www.diu.edu/

As of the repository's initial build (2026-08-09), DIU's public distance-course page describes:
- **AL4302** as a 3-credit undergraduate course using IPA to identify, pronounce, and transcribe speech sounds and suprasegmentals, describe speech production, and introduce basic acoustic analysis.
- **AL4303** as a 3-credit undergraduate course in phonological analysis.
- **AL4410** as a 4-credit undergraduate course in morphological and syntactic analysis.
- **AA4305** as a 3-credit undergraduate course in learner-directed language and culture acquisition with a native-speaker lab component.

Always re-check DIU before treating scheduling or requirements as current.

## Credit-hour workload

The 45-hours-per-credit planning convention is used as a conservative independent-study design target, not as a claim that DIU will award credit. U.S. Department of Education guidance describes a semester credit hour as work reasonably approximating one hour of instruction plus at least two hours of out-of-class work per week for about 15 weeks, or an equivalent amount of work over another format.

- U.S. Department of Education credit-hour guidance: https://www.ed.gov/laws-and-policy/higher-education-laws-and-policy/program-integrity-information-questions-and-12
